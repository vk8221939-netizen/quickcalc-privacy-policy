Privacy Policy for QuickCalc Calculator

Last updated: October 4, 2026

QuickCalc Calculator respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use the QuickCalc Calculator app.

Information We Collect

QuickCalc Calculator does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information with us or with third parties.

The app does not require you to create an account and does not require internet access to perform calculations.

Calculator History

QuickCalc Calculator may store your calculation history locally on your device so that you can view previous calculations. This information remains on your device and is not sent to our servers.

Permissions

QuickCalc Calculator does not require sensitive device permissions such as location, contacts, camera, microphone, or storage access for its normal operation.

Third-Party Services

QuickCalc Calculator does not use third-party advertising or analytics services that collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

QuickCalc Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Data Security

Because the app does not transmit personal information to our servers, there is no personal information maintained by us through the app. Information stored locally on your device is protected by the security mechanisms of your device and operating system.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or QuickCalc Calculator, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
